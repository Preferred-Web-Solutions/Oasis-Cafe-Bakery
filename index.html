Our story

OASIS CAFE AND BAKERY brings the rich tastes of Uzbekistan and beyond to Loveland. Our menu celebrates authentic Uzbek classics like plov and shurpa alongside Tajik dishes and Eastern European comfort foods.

We use quality ingredients to create flavorful halal meals in a welcoming space where families and friends can gather for breakfast, lunch, or dinner.
